Invitatus · you were given a card

A card was placed
in your hand.

Then someone has already decided about you. That is the whole of the mechanism — there is nothing here to apply to.

Go back to the person who gave it to you and say that you have read this. What follows is a conversation, in a room, at a table. It is not a form and it will not be one.

— the card is the introduction; the talk is the door —
Sex vota · what will be asked of you
i.
I shall live within material limits.
ii.
I shall maintain targeted radical transparency.
iii.
I shall practice radical localism in goods and services.
iv.
I shall strive, without ceasing, to master practical skills of local use — one is the laughable minimum.
v.
I shall reject the convenience ethic.
vi.
I shall reject the attention economy and its false promises.

Sworn at entry, attested annually, without ceremony.
