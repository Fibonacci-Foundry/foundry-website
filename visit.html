Come sit
at the table.

We gather once a month in the Appalachian Mountains. You don't need to be a member or bring anything with you.

What a gathering is like

  • A shared meal. We start by eating together. Bring a dish if you like.
  • An hour of work. Someone teaches a practical skill, or we build something a neighbor needs. Watch or join in.
  • Conversation. About the teachings we follow, the work, and how life is going. Say as much or as little as you want.

How to come

Write to us and we'll send the date, the place and directions. We're a small community and like to know who's coming.

Questions are welcome too.

Write to us hello at fibonaccifoundry dot org
