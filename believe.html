Credo · I believe

What we believe

We follow the teachings of a carpenter who taught people to share what they had and care for their neighbors.

Our creed is short, and we try to live it out in how we work and spend our days.

i.

Balance in all things. There is a peace where mind, body and spirit are at rest together. We will not reach it perfectly in this life, but our fulfillment lies in seeking it.

ii.

Nothing comes without cost. To make something, something else is given up. We accept the seasons of life and death rather than trying to cheat them.

iii.

Work is sacred. Every workshop is a holy place of making. Each of us has gifts, and we are called to find them and to grow them.

iv.

Things have weight. What we own takes up room in our lives. Too much of it weighs us down and pulls us toward wanting more.

v.

Wealth is dangerous. Money can do great good or great harm. Chasing it is a quick road to bondage.

How we hold ourselves accountable

These rules are written into how the Foundry is run:

  • Leaders serve limited terms. No one leads a congregation for more than eight years, so the church never becomes about one person.
  • The books are open. Every member may see what the church takes in, what it spends, and what its leaders are paid.
  • Our buildings stay humble. Plain, useful rooms, made from local materials where we can.
